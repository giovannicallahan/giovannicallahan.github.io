Which one is the best value?

My current experiment asks when people stop weighing their options and reach for a rule of thumb. You pick the best value among four brands of an everyday product, such as pasta, bread or laundry detergent, while holding something in mind: a few digits, a string of letters, or a pattern of squares.

Works on a phone or a computer.

What to expect

  • A short practice firstSix practice trials with feedback, so the rule is clear before anything counts.
  • Three versionsNumber memory: hold a few digits while choosing household products. Pattern memory: hold squares on a grid while choosing food products. Letter memory: hold a string of letters while choosing other everyday goods, such as pens and umbrellas. Every brand name is made up.
  • Two parts of 44 choicesOne part has a small memory load on each trial and the other a larger one.
  • Anonymous responsesThe task does not ask for your name or email. Your choices and their timing are saved under a random code when you finish.
  • Just looking?The one-minute demo runs a few trials and saves nothing. A full session saves your responses.
