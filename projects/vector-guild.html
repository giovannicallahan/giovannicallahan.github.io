MathCosmos

A maths game that starts from zero. Choose a realm on a 3D star map, work through its missions, and every first solve builds another part of your outpost. It was called Vector Guild until version 2.0.

Play MathCosmos

Free. No account. Runs in your browser.

What's inside

  • Two realms, 228 missionsFoundations (40 concepts from place value to functions, each with three practice missions in new settings, 160 in all) needs no maths background. Linear algebra (68 missions) follows. Nine more realms are on the map and not built yet.
  • Collect the people behind the mathsSolving missions earns stardust. Spend it to find 48 mathematicians, physicists and computing pioneers, each with a short checked biography. No real money, ever.
  • One calculation at a timeEach mission has a purpose, a lesson and a worked example, ordered so every idea builds on the last.
  • Hints, guided steps and labsA graph lab and a 3D bench let you see the vectors before you commit to an answer.
  • Linked to the sourceFoundations missions point to the matching OpenStax section, and linear algebra missions to the MIT OpenCourseWare 18.06 lecture.
  • Your progress stays with youThe game saves in your browser, and you can export a backup from its settings.
