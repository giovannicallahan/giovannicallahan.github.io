Vector Guild

A linear algebra adventure. Work through 68 engineering missions, and every first solve builds another part of your outpost.

Play Vector Guild

Free. No account. Runs in your browser.

What's inside

  • 68 missions in eight chaptersEach mission is one calculation with a purpose, ordered so every idea builds on the last.
  • Hints, guided steps and labsA graph lab and a 3D bench let you see the vectors before you commit to an answer.
  • Linked to the sourceMissions point to the matching MIT OpenCourseWare 18.06 lecture.
  • Your progress stays with youThe game saves in your browser, and you can export a backup from its settings.
