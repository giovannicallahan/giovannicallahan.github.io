Tools

Three free tools I built for research and learning. Each runs in a browser, with no account.

  • Gyrafold

    A 3D atlas of 300+ brain structures. Tap a structure to see what it does, then quiz yourself.

    • 3D atlas
    • Free, no account

  • CogViz Research Studio

    A planner for psychology experiments: compare designs, plan the analysis and export the plan.

    • Python
    • Streamlit

  • MathCosmos

    A maths game that starts from zero. Pick a realm on a 3D star map and build an outpost as you solve its missions.

    • JavaScript
    • Free, no account
